Well… I'm a Java Champion now. 🥳

I still need a second to let that sink in. [When the news landed](https://x.com/Java_Champions/status/2101964046878568483), I did the classic dance: read the email twice, smiled like an idiot, then immediately wanted to tell someone. So here I am, telling you.

<!--more-->

I'm genuinely thrilled. Not in a polished press-release way — more in a "wait, me?" way. This program is full of people I've looked up to for years, people I've shared stages with, people I've learned from in hallway chats after a JUG meetup. [Landing in that circle](https://javachampions.org/members.html) feels surreal and wonderful at the same time.

If there's a through-line in what I do, it's Java and the joy of shipping useful things with other humans. [FSCrawler](https://fscrawler.readthedocs.io/) is still the project I started years ago because I needed to index files into Elasticsearch and thought others might too. Along the way I've also had fun contributing around the ecosystem — things like the Elasticsearch module for [Testcontainers](https://java.testcontainers.org/), or helping keep [LangChain4j](https://docs.langchain4j.info/)'s Elasticsearch integration in good shape. None of that was a grand plan to collect badges. It was just… building stuff, fixing stuff, and sharing it.

And then there's the community side. Speaking at JUGs and conferences, live-coding without a safety net, meeting developers who are stuck on the same problems I was stuck on ten years ago. That's the part that still gives me energy. Open source without people is just a repo. The rooms, the coffee breaks, the "can you show me that again?" moments — that's where it becomes real.

So thank you! ❤️

To the folks who nominated me: you know who you are. To everyone who voted with so much enthusiasm: I felt that, and it means a lot.

Funny detail: this whole "you should join the Java Champions" conversation actually started during a [DJ set at Volcamp](https://djdadoo.pilato.fr/mixes/2025/2025-10-02-volcamp-2025-full-set-raw/). Yeah — sharing in another way… music this time. 😉

I'll keep showing up — writing code, giving talks, helping where I can. Same David. Just with a slightly wider grin today.
